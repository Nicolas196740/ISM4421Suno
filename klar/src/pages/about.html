About us

Building the homes South Florida neighborhoods need

KLAR Construction is a residential real estate developer. We buy land and build duplexes, triplexes, townhomes and small multifamily projects in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties.

Our mission

Well-built homes, on the right lots, at fair prices

South Florida has a housing shortage, and much of it sits in the "missing middle": homes bigger than an apartment and more attainable than a single-family house. That's exactly what we build.

By focusing on small infill projects of 2 to 12 units, we can move quickly, keep quality high, and add homes to neighborhoods that already have the schools, jobs and amenities people want.
