What we look for

Lots that can hold 2–4+ homes

  • Location: Miami-Dade, Broward or Palm Beach County
  • Zoning: duplex, triplex, townhome or multifamily (for example RD, RM, R-2, R-3, or similar), or land that could be rezoned
  • Size: typically 5,000 sq ft and up
  • Condition: vacant lots, tear-downs and older single-family homes all welcome
  • Situation: inherited land, out-of-state owners, liens or title issues; we've seen it before
