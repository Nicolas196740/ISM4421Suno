Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

KLAR Construction ("KLAR," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you fill out a form on our website, we collect the information you choose to give us, which may include:

  • Your name, email address, phone number and preferred contact method
  • Whether you're a home buyer, investor, land owner or other
  • Details about your inquiry, such as budget range, desired area, timeline, financing status, investment range and experience, or a property's address, lot size, zoning and asking price
  • Your message, how you heard about us, and your contact and text-message consent choices

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also automatically log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, for security and to keep the site running.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and follow up about the projects, investments or land sale you asked about
  • To send you updates about current and future KLAR projects that match your interests
  • To evaluate properties submitted to us
  • To keep records of our communications and improve our website and services
  • To prevent spam and fraud, and to comply with the law

Phone calls, emails and text messages

By checking the required consent box, you agree that KLAR Construction may contact you by phone or email about your inquiry. Text messages are sent only if you separately check the optional text-message box. Agreeing to texts is not a condition of doing business with us. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email or by contacting us.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or text-messaging consent with third parties for their marketing purposes.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run this website and our business, such as website hosting, database storage and email delivery. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • With partners you ask us to connect you with, such as a lender, title company or real estate professional, and only with your permission.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of KLAR, our customers or others.
  • As part of a merger, sale or transfer of our business, subject to this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and follow up on opportunities, and as required for legal and business records. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

How we protect it

Form submissions are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and stored in a secured database that only authorized KLAR staff can access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.
