Our story

Small projects. Big attention to detail.

KLAR Construction started with a simple idea: South Florida needs more well-built homes that working families and investors can actually afford. Large builders chase 300-unit communities. We focus on the infill lots in established neighborhoods, where a thoughtfully designed duplex, triplex or row of townhomes can add real value.

We handle every step in-house, from buying the land and pulling permits to construction and sale, so we control quality, timelines and cost.

More about us
  • Land to keys, in-house. Acquisition, design, permitting and construction under one roof.
  • Built for Florida. Impact windows and doors, concrete block construction, and current wind-load code.
  • Infill locations. Close to jobs, schools and transit in neighborhoods people already love.
  • Straight answers. Clear timelines and honest numbers for buyers, investors and sellers.
