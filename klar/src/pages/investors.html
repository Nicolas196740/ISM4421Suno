How we work

From land to lease-up

We run every project the same disciplined way, so you always know where your money is and what happens next.

  1. Find and underwrite the land

    We target infill lots zoned for 2–4+ units and check zoning, comps and rents before we buy.

  2. Design and permit

    Our architects and engineers design for the lot, the market and Florida's building code.

  3. Build

    Our team manages construction to a fixed schedule and budget, with regular investor updates.

  4. Sell, lease or refinance

    We exit by selling units, selling the property to an investor, or leasing up and refinancing.
